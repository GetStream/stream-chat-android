AIUIEntrance
Link copied to clipboard
@Composable
fun AIUIEntrance(modifier: Modifier = Modifier, content: @Composable () -> Unit)

Brings a part of a reply's generated UI in when it shows, such as when the agent writes it: the part fades in, rising into place and coming into focus, after the parts that came in just before it. What shows together with the reply's UI, or with a part around it, comes in with that, so a reply written before it showed shows at once. With the system's animations off, parts show at once.
