The steps an AI agent took while replying, in order. Show it before the reply's text.

AIMessageParts(parts = parts)
Content copied to clipboard

Each step shows through AIMessagePartItem: reasoning, tool calls, and a neutral placeholder for kinds this SDK doesn't know. With an AIToolApprover, a call waiting for this person's approval shows its question under it, and with its AIToolAuthorizer, a call waiting for them to sign in shows the sign-in, through ChatAiComponentFactory.AIToolAuthorizationContent. To show some steps your own way, such as reasoning you stream separately or a kind of your own, use the overload that takes content and fall back to AIMessagePartItem for the rest.
